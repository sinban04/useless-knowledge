How the Immune System Works

A layered decision system for finding danger, containing it fast, learning its molecular signature, and remembering it — without attacking the self. Built around four cells with four different logics of recognition: macrophages, B cells, T cells, and NK cells.

🧭The escalation pipeline — breach to memory

Each chapter zooms into one station of this sequence. Read it left to right.

STAGE 0
Barrier
Skin, mucus, acid, and microbiota keep most microbes out.
STAGE 1
Innate sensing
PRRs and complement recognize conserved danger patterns.
STAGE 2
Inflammation
Cytokines and chemokines recruit cells and change the tissue.
STAGE 3
Antigen presentation
Dendritic cells carry peptide evidence to lymph nodes.
STAGE 4
Clonal expansion
Rare matching B and T cells are selected and cloned.
STAGE 5
Effectors
Antibodies, CD8 killing, and activated macrophages clear it.
STAGE 6
Memory
Long-lived B and T cells make the next response faster.

Broad innate sensing (left) gates narrow clonal expansion (right): the speed of stages 0–2 buys time and decides whether the precise but slower adaptive stages 3–6 fire at all.

The one idea to keep The best mental model is not "innate vs adaptive" or "weak vs strong." It is broad danger-sensing gates narrow clonal expansion: fast innate cells decide whether a precise, slow, and potentially dangerous adaptive response should be unleashed at all.

4Four cells, four logics of recognition

Each cell asks a different question. They do not duplicate one another — they cover each other's blind spots.

🍽️
Innate sentinel
Macrophage
  • "Is there something to eat, alarm about, or repair?"
  • Pattern receptors, Fc & complement receptors
  • Phagocytosis · cytokines · antigen display · repair
🏹
Adaptive · humoral
B cell
  • "Does my antibody-shaped receptor bind this target?"
  • Unique BCR made by gene rearrangement
  • Antibodies · plasma cells · memory B cells
🎯
Adaptive · cellular
T cell
  • "Is this displayed peptide dangerous, in the right context?"
  • Reads peptide–MHC, never free antigen
  • CD8 killing · CD4 help · regulation
⚖️
Innate lymphocyte
NK cell
  • "Does this cell look stressed or missing normal self?"
  • Balance of activating & inhibitory receptors
  • Missing-self killing · IFN-γ · ADCC
