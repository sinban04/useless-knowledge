# References

This report draws from immunology textbooks, NCBI Bookshelf chapters, NIH/NIAID educational material, Nobel background material, and primary literature. The sources are grouped by how they support the chapters.

---

## Textbook Foundations

- **Janeway's Immunobiology**, 5th ed. (Janeway, Travers, Walport, Shlomchik). Open version via NCBI Bookshelf. Core source for innate-adaptive architecture, antigen presentation, lymphocyte activation, complement, antibody responses, and immunological memory.
- **The Immune System**, 5th ed. (Peter Parham). Clear conceptual explanation of MHC, lymphocyte development, antibody class switching, and NK-cell recognition.
- **Cellular and Molecular Immunology**, 10th ed. (Abbas, Lichtman, Pillai). Strong for clinical framing, tolerance, hypersensitivity, and immune-mediated disease.
- **Kuby Immunology**, 8th ed. (Punt, Stranford, Jones, Owen). Useful cross-check for cell lineages, cytokines, and immune response timelines.

## Primary and Review Literature

- **Janeway CA Jr (1989).** "Approaching the asymptote? Evolution and revolution in immunology." *Cold Spring Harbor Symposia on Quantitative Biology* 54:1-13. Pattern-recognition framing for innate control of adaptive immunity.
- **Medzhitov R, Preston-Hurlburt P, Janeway CA Jr (1997).** "A human homologue of the Drosophila Toll protein signals activation of adaptive immunity." *Nature* 388:394-397. Toll-like receptor signaling connection to adaptive immunity.
- **Medzhitov R (2008).** "Origin and physiological roles of inflammation." *Nature* 454:428-435. Inflammation as a physiological response program.
- **Matzinger P (1994).** "Tolerance, danger, and the extended family." *Annual Review of Immunology* 12:991-1045. Danger model perspective.
- **Steinman RM, Cohn ZA (1973).** "Identification of a novel cell type in peripheral lymphoid organs of mice. I. Morphology, quantitation, tissue distribution." *Journal of Experimental Medicine* 137:1142-1162. Dendritic-cell discovery.
- **Doherty PC, Zinkernagel RM (1975).** "Enhanced immunological surveillance in mice heterozygous at the H-2 gene complex." *Nature* 256:50-52. MHC restriction of T-cell recognition.
- **Rudolph MG, Stanfield RL, Wilson IA (2006).** "How TCRs bind MHCs, peptides, and coreceptors." *Annual Review of Immunology* 24:419-466. Structural basis of the diagonal TCR docking geometry, CDR loop usage, and coreceptor contacts (chapter 6, peptide-MHC–TCR binding).
- **McKeithan TW (1995).** "Kinetic proofreading in T-cell receptor signal transduction." *PNAS* 92:5042-5046. Origin of the kinetic-proofreading model for how bond dwell time gates TCR signaling.
- **Valitutti S, Müller S, Cella M, Padovan E, Lanzavecchia A (1995).** "Serial triggering of many T-cell receptors by a few peptide-MHC complexes." *Nature* 375:148-151. Evidence that brief, low-affinity TCR-pMHC bonds allow serial engagement and high sensitivity.
- **Palmer DB (2013).** "The effect of age on thymic function." *Frontiers in Immunology* 4:316. Review source for thymic involution, reduced naive T-cell output, and reduced TCR diversity with age.
- **Gudmundsdottir J, Soderling J, Berggren H, et al. (2018).** "Long-term clinical effects of early thymectomy: associations with autoimmune diseases, cancer, infections, and atopic diseases." *Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology* 141:2294-2297.e8. Cohort evidence linking thymectomy before age five with later immune outcomes.
- **Halnon NJ, Jamieson B, Plunkett M, Kitchen CMR, Pham T, Krogstad P (2005).** "Thymic function and impaired maintenance of peripheral T cell populations in children with congenital heart disease and surgical thymectomy." *Pediatric Research* 57:42-48. Evidence that early thymectomy can reduce naive T-cell maintenance and alter peripheral T-cell populations.
- **Wherry EJ, Kurachi M (2015).** "Molecular and cellular insights into T cell exhaustion." *Nature Reviews Immunology* 15:486-499. Canonical review of the exhaustion program, the hierarchical loss of function, and inhibitory-receptor co-expression (chapter 7, T-cell exhaustion).
- **Khan O, Giles JR, McDonald S, et al. (2019).** "TOX transcriptionally and epigenetically programs CD8+ T cell exhaustion." *Nature* 571:211-218. Identifies TOX as the transcription factor that establishes and maintains the exhaustion state.
- **Im SJ, Hashimoto M, Gerner MY, et al. (2016).** "Defining CD8+ T cells that provide the proliferative burst after PD-1 therapy." *Nature* 537:417-421. Stem-like TCF1+ progenitor-exhausted cells as the subset that responds to PD-1 blockade.
- **Sadelain M, Brentjens R, Rivière I (2013).** "The basic principles of chimeric antigen receptor design." *Cancer Discovery* 3:388-398. Foundational account of CAR ectodomain/endodomain architecture and generations (chapter 7, CAR-T cells).
- **June CH, Sadelain M (2018).** "Chimeric Antigen Receptor Therapy." *New England Journal of Medicine* 379:64-73. Clinical and mechanistic review of CAR-T therapy, manufacturing, and toxicity.
- **Maude SL, Laetsch TW, Buechner J, et al. (2018).** "Tisagenlecleucel in children and young adults with B-cell lymphoblastic leukemia." *New England Journal of Medicine* 378:439-448. Pivotal ELIANA trial of CD19 CAR-T in relapsed/refractory B-ALL.
- **Bargou R, Leo E, Zugmaier G, et al. (2008).** "Tumor regression in cancer patients by very low doses of a T cell-engaging antibody." *Science* 321:974-977. First-in-human proof of concept for the BiTE mechanism (blinatumomab; chapter 7, bispecific T-cell engagers).
- **Kantarjian H, Stein A, Gökbuget N, et al. (2017).** "Blinatumomab versus chemotherapy for advanced acute lymphoblastic leukemia." *New England Journal of Medicine* 376:836-847. Pivotal TOWER trial establishing the CD19×CD3 BiTE in relapsed/refractory B-ALL.
- **Nathan P, Hassel JC, Rutkowski P, et al. (2021).** "Overall survival benefit with tebentafusp in metastatic uveal melanoma." *New England Journal of Medicine* 385:1196-1206. A TCR-based (peptide-MHC-targeting) T-cell engager, showing the class extending to solid tumors.
- **Tonegawa S (1983).** "Somatic generation of antibody diversity." *Nature* 302:575-581. Immunoglobulin gene rearrangement and antibody diversity.
- **Burnet FM (1957).** "A modification of Jerne's theory of antibody production using the concept of clonal selection." *Australian Journal of Science* 20:67-69. Clonal selection theory.
- **Karre K, Ljunggren HG, Piontek G, Kiessling R (1986).** "Selective rejection of H-2-deficient lymphoma variants suggests alternative immune defence strategy." *Nature* 319:675-678. Original missing-self NK-cell recognition evidence.
- **Ljunggren HG, Karre K (1990).** "In search of the missing self: MHC molecules and NK cell recognition." *Immunology Today* 11:237-244. Missing-self framework.
- **Lanier LL (2005).** "NK cell recognition." *Annual Review of Immunology* 23:225-274. NK receptor balance and activation/inhibition review.
- **Kim S, Poursine-Laurent J, Truscott SM, et al. (2005).** "Licensing of natural killer cells by host major histocompatibility complex class I molecules." *Nature* 436:709-713. Foundational NK-cell licensing / education evidence.
- **Anfossi N, Andre P, Guia S, et al. (2006).** "Human NK cell education by inhibitory receptors for MHC class I." *Immunity* 25:331-342. Human KIR/self-MHC education evidence.
- **Raulet DH, Vance RE (2006).** "Self-tolerance of natural killer cells." *Nature Reviews Immunology* 6:520-531. Review of NK education, licensing, and self-tolerance models.
- **Hargreaves DC, Hyman PL, Lu TT, et al. (2001).** "A coordinated change in chemokine responsiveness guides plasma cell movements." *Journal of Experimental Medicine* 194:45-56. CXCR4/CXCL12-guided plasma-cell homing to bone marrow.
- **Belnoue E, Tougne C, Rochat AF, et al. (2012).** "Homing and adhesion patterns determine the cellular composition of the bone marrow plasma cell niche." *Journal of Immunology* 188:1283-1291. Bone-marrow plasma-cell niche positioning and adhesion/survival context.
- **Sakaguchi S et al. (1995).** "Immunologic self-tolerance maintained by activated T cells expressing IL-2 receptor alpha-chains (CD25)." *Journal of Immunology* 155:1151-1164. Foundational regulatory T-cell paper.
- **Ishida Y et al. (1992).** "Induced expression of PD-1, a novel member of the immunoglobulin gene superfamily, upon programmed cell death." *EMBO Journal* 11:3887-3895. PD-1 discovery.
- **Leach DR, Krummel MF, Allison JP (1996).** "Enhancement of antitumor immunity by CTLA-4 blockade." *Science* 271:1734-1736. Checkpoint blockade proof of principle.
