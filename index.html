  3. 2026-05-23 KR Jaehoss

    First-Principles Chemistry — Four Bonds, One Well

    Octet rule? Forget it. A first-principles rebuild of chemistry from two ideas — Coulomb's law and energy minimization. The four kinds of chemical bond (ionic, covalent, metallic, intermolecular) turn out to be one potential well in four shapes; and four everyday questions — why diamond is the hardest natural material, why salt cleaves cleanly, why copper draws into wire, why water freezes at 0 and boils at 100 — all fall out of the same graph. Atom → electron → bond → molecule, across 10 chapters plus a slide deck.
